# Live Feedback Preview: research question

Once a pedestrian's map app has brought them to the right block, can live on-device reading of the street (door numbers and signs) guide them to the destination's door more often than the current photo-and-panorama approach, without misleading them?

## Sub-questions

1. **Feasibility:** how often can a phone read a door number or sign live from the sidewalk, and from how far? (#106)
2. **Effectiveness:** is the share of Field tests Guided to the door higher than the current app's at the same destinations? (#121, #123)
3. **Safety:** does the share of Field tests that end Misled stay at zero?
4. **Usability:** do blind and low-vision users find the guidance intuitive? (User tests, after the safety rules in #124 are approved)

## What counts as an answer

- Both apps run paired Field tests at the same destinations, alternating which app goes first.
- The number of Field tests per app is set once the first experiment (#106) shows what is feasible.
- The Preview answers yes if its share Guided to the door is clearly higher than the current app's and it has no Misled runs.
- A single Misled run stops testing until it has been investigated.
- What answers yes moves into the main app; otherwise the Preview retires.
